Low Stock Urgency Badges
When two of something are left, the shop should say so — quietly, in the same place on every page, and without repeating the stock line the customer has already read.
website_sale_stockProduct · category · websiteOPL-1
The sellable number
Ten on the shelf with eight already promised is two left, and that is what the badge says. On-hand stock is not the number a customer can buy.
One rule, three surfaces
Product page, shop grid and cart all read the same threshold and the same sentence, so a customer never sees two different counts for one product.
An urgent second wording
A tighter threshold can switch the badge to a second sentence — a number at five, just "Last few" at two.
Where the threshold comes from
One number, in three places. A value of 0 is not "off"; it is "ask the next place".
ProductOn the product form under Sales › Website Low Stock Badge. Wins whenever it is above zero.
Public categoryOn Website › Configuration › Product Categories. The nearest category with a value answers; its parents are next, then the site.
WebsiteOn the website form, under Low Stock Badge. Also holds the two sentences and the urgent threshold.
What a customer sees
✓On the product page the badge sits under the availability line, from the same variant information the page already renders
✓On the shop grid it appears under the product name, on the tile whose first variant is low
✓In the cart each line states its own variant's count, so two sizes of one product can show two different numbers
✓The sentence is per website and translatable; {qty} is replaced with the number, and the urgent sentence may leave it out
What it will not say
Not trackedA service or a consumable that does not manage stock never carries a scarcity badge, whatever its category says.
Above the thresholdAt the threshold the badge appears; one unit above it, nothing. There is no "almost low" state.
Nothing leftZero is the out-of-stock line's business. "Only 0 left" is not a scarcity message, it is the sentence above it with a number.
The same words twiceIf the configured badge would repeat the stock sentence word for word, it is dropped instead of printed twice.
Threshold zeroA site that sets no threshold gets no badge at all — no extra markup, no empty element on the page.
